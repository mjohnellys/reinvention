12-week ramp-up

Habit design rules

  1. Attach to anchors. After breakfast, walk. After arriving home, change into training clothes.
  2. Prepare the environment. Clothes out, book visible, next app task written down.
  3. Reduce friction. Close gym, repeatable meals, fixed review time.
  4. Floor, not ceiling. The minimum counts. Consistency is the target.
  5. Never punish a miss. No doubling up. Resume at the next scheduled opportunity.
  6. Protect sleep. Don't trade sleep for habits. Get qualified advice for pain or injury.

The rest of the list

Parked until the foundation is stable: influencer targets, book, cold-approach quotas, extra business ideas, car and house hunting.

Dating: stay open, but no weekly KPI. Prioritize real-world social life and readiness.
